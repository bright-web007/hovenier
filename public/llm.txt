## Website Overview
Hovenier.frl is a comprehensive Dutch landscaping service directory and lead generation platform specifically focused on Friesland, Netherlands. The website connects homeowners with professional gardeners (hoveniers) and landscaping specialists throughout all 11 municipalities in Friesland.

## Business Model
- Primary Function: Lead generation for landscaping professionals
- Revenue Model: Commission-based leads and premium listings
- Target Market: Homeowners in Friesland seeking gardening and landscaping services
- Service Provider Network: Vetted hoveniers and landscaping companies
